Cheng-Kuang Chang

Undergraduate researcher @ MIT CSAIL SLS

I’m an MIT undergraduate (Class of 2029), working in the Spoken Language Systems Group at CSAIL, advised by Dr. James Glass and Dr. Kai-Wei Chang.

My research focuses on full-duplex models, audio generation, and speech understanding. I’m also part of the MIT–IBM Computing Research Lab’s Sight and Sound project.

I study Electrical Engineering with Computing, with a GPA of 5.0/5.0. I was a gold medalist at both the International Physics Olympiad (IPhO) and the Asian Physics Olympiad (APhO) in 2023.
